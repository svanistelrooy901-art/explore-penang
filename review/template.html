This draft uses extracted OpenStreetMap geometry. The depth view adds raised land, decorative vegetation and landmark symbols to the same coordinates. It is an early art study; it does not represent measured terrain.

Regional coverage is cropped. The eastern edge of the land shape is Penang’s administrative boundary, not an island coastline.

Before publishing: Mamu reviews the coastline, both bridge landings, ferry alignment and representative attraction pins. The final detailed illustrated-world treatment follows that approval.
